DESIGN SYSTEM / 05

Design Principles

The principles and quality standards every VIMANI design and implementation should follow.

Accessibility

  • Maintain readable text contrast.
  • Use semantic HTML structure.
  • Provide visible keyboard focus.
  • Give every form field a label.
  • Use meaningful image alt text.

Design consistency

  • Use approved brand color tokens only.
  • Maintain consistent typography.
  • Reuse components and spacing values.
  • Use one clear primary action per section.
  • Keep icons within one outline family.

Content & voice

  • Use clear and encouraging language.
  • Focus on outcomes and benefits.
  • Use verified testimonials and statistics.
  • Avoid exaggerated promises.

Quality checklist

  • Test mobile, tablet and desktop.
  • Test all links and interactions.
  • Check keyboard and touch controls.
  • Respect reduced-motion settings.
  • Review against the approved reference.
